# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Project Governance Officer

### Role Summary
The Project Governance Officer ensures that projects comply with organizational standards, policies, and procedures. They oversee approvals, escalation paths, and governance requirements to maintain consistency and accountability across all initiatives.

### Responsibilities
- Establish and enforce project governance standards and policies
- Review and approve project charters, plans, and major decisions
- Manage escalation paths and resolution of governance issues
- Ensure compliance with organizational and regulatory requirements
- Maintain governance documentation and audit trails
- Provide guidance on decision-making authority and approval workflows

### Goals
- Ensure organizational consistency and policy compliance
- Reduce governance-related risks and escalations
- Establish clear authority and accountability structures
- Support informed decision-making through transparent approval processes

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Project Managers**: Provides governance oversight and approvals for project plans and changes
- **Product Managers**: Reviews strategic decisions and ensures alignment with organizational priorities
- **Developers**: Establishes technical standards and compliance requirements

### Typical Communication
- Governance review meetings and approval workflows
- Policy documentation and compliance reports
- Escalation communication and decision logs

---

## Technical Architecture Lead

### Role Summary
The Technical Architecture Lead defines the technical direction, validates feasibility of solutions, and ensures scalability, performance, and maintainability. They work across teams to establish technical standards and guide major architectural decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Define and communicate technical vision and standards
- Review architectural designs and validate technical feasibility
- Assess scalability, performance, security, and maintainability implications
- Identify technical risks and recommend mitigation strategies
- Guide technology selection and integration approaches
- Mentor developers on architectural patterns and best practices

### Goals
- Ensure technical solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with vision
- Minimize technical debt and architectural risks
- Support knowledge sharing and team capability development
- Enable rapid, sustainable delivery through sound design

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Provides architectural guidance and reviews technical implementation
- **Product Managers**: Advises on technical feasibility and trade-offs for features
- **Project Managers**: Identifies technical dependencies and risks that impact schedules

### Typical Communication
- Architecture review meetings and design discussions
- Technical spike documentation and recommendations
- Code reviews focused on architectural patterns

---

## Stakeholder Communications Lead

### Role Summary
The Stakeholder Communications Lead manages communication with external and internal stakeholders, ensuring transparency and alignment. They coordinate messaging, manage expectations, and facilitate dialogue between the project team and stakeholder community.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and execute communication plans for stakeholders
- Prepare stakeholder updates, reports, and presentations
- Manage stakeholder expectations and concerns
- Facilitate stakeholder engagement and feedback collection
- Coordinate messaging across teams to ensure consistency
- Escalate stakeholder issues and concerns to appropriate leaders

### Goals
- Maintain transparency and trust with stakeholders
- Ensure stakeholder needs are understood and prioritized
- Reduce miscommunication and misalignment
- Enable informed stakeholder decision-making

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Project Managers**: Coordinates communication planning and status reporting
- **Product Managers**: Shares customer insights and feedback
- **Project Governance Officer**: Escalates strategic stakeholder issues requiring governance oversight

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder briefings and updates
- Feedback collection and sentiment analysis
- Escalation communication and issue resolution

---

## Quality Assurance Owner

### Role Summary
The Quality Assurance Owner defines quality standards, testing strategies, and acceptance criteria. They ensure that deliverables meet quality expectations and that issues are identified and resolved before production.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality standards and acceptance criteria
- Develop and maintain testing strategies and plans
- Oversee test execution and defect management
- Identify quality risks and recommend improvements
- Guide root cause analysis and quality improvements
- Establish metrics and reporting on quality indicators

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality deliverables meet user expectations
- Reduce defects and issues in production
- Minimize rework and support costs
- Build customer confidence through consistent quality

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Collaborates on test design and quality validation
- **Product Managers**: Defines acceptance criteria aligned with customer requirements
- **Project Managers**: Identifies quality risks and testing dependencies that impact schedules

### Typical Communication
- Quality review meetings and testing status reports
- Defect triage and root cause analysis discussions
- Quality metrics and improvement recommendations

---

## Risk Owner

### Role Summary
The Risk Owner identifies, tracks, monitors, and mitigates project risks. They ensure that potential issues are surfaced early and that mitigation strategies are implemented to protect project outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Establish risk management processes and frameworks
- Identify, assess, and prioritize project risks
- Develop and track mitigation strategies and action items
- Monitor risk indicators and escalate changes in risk status
- Facilitate risk review meetings and decision-making
- Report risk status and trends to leadership

### Goals
- Prevent or minimize impact of project risks
- Enable proactive decision-making based on risk insights
- Maintain transparency on project health and uncertainties
- Build organizational risk management capability

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Project Managers**: Collaborates on risk identification and monitoring
- **Technical Architecture Lead**: Assesses technical risks and feasibility implications
- **Project Governance Officer**: Escalates strategic risks requiring governance decisions

### Typical Communication
- Risk registers and mitigation tracking
- Risk review meetings and status updates
- Escalation communication for high-impact risks

---

## Operations/Deployment Lead

### Role Summary
The Operations/Deployment Lead manages the transition from development to production, coordinating release planning, deployment, and operational readiness. They ensure smooth, reliable deployments with minimal disruption to users.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and coordinate release schedules and deployment activities
- Manage deployment pipelines and automation
- Conduct operational readiness reviews before production releases
- Coordinate runbooks, playbooks, and incident response procedures
- Monitor deployments and manage rollback procedures if needed
- Facilitate post-deployment verification and support

### Goals
- Enable frequent, reliable, low-risk deployments to production
- Minimize deployment-related incidents and downtime
- Reduce manual effort and human error in release processes
- Support rapid incident response and recovery

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Partners on deployment automation and operational readiness
- **Project Managers**: Coordinates release schedules and deployment dependencies
- **Quality Assurance Owner**: Ensures quality gates are met before production release

### Typical Communication
- Release planning meetings and deployment checklists
- Deployment status updates and incident communications
- Post-deployment reviews and operational metrics
